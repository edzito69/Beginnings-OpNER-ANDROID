# Beginnings-OpNER-ANDROID

Beginnings OpNER brings EarthBound Beginnings Remake to Android with portrait and landscape gameplay, touch and gamepad controls, pinch zoom, save states, CRT filters, fast-forward and rewind. Created by Edzito for the ROM hack by the EBBR Team and PK Hack. An fully independent, unaffiliated project. No ROM is included.

Android features

Play EarthBound Beginnings Remake on your phone, with layouts adapted for both portrait and landscape gameplay.
- Touch movement: hold and drag in the direction you want to walk.
- Swipe navigation for menus, with central taps to confirm and a lower Back area.
- Pinch-to-zoom camera controls.
- Dedicated OK, Back and Actions buttons in landscape mode.
- Optional virtual buttons and physical gamepad support.
- Save states with screenshot previews, quicksave/load phone icons and autosaves.
- Fast-forward and rewind, with an option to hide their on-screen buttons.
- Customizable CRT filters and four personal presets.
- Mirrored psychedelic battle backgrounds that fill the portrait display.
- Select your own ROM through Android’s file picker on first launch.

Supports 60 and 30 FPS presentation modes. Actual performance depends on your device.
