# Beginnings OpNER — Credits

OpNER by Edzito

EarthBound Beginnings Remake

Independent emulator adaptation
I am not affiliated with the EBBR team, Nintendo or SHIGESATO ITOI/HOBONICHI. I respect the original team's work. I created this dedicated emulator for the EarthBound Beginnings ROM on PC and Android, aiming to make a good experience accessible to everyone: 60 FPS emulation, expanded 16:9 scenery, 1080p output, quality-of-life options, and keyboard, gamepad, mouse and touchscreen controls. Actual performance depends on the device. All remake credits belong to its creators.

History
This fan remake recreates EarthBound Beginnings using EarthBound's SNES engine. The team released the remake in June 2026 and version 1.2 on July 2, 2026. Their development notes describe H.S's earlier research and its contribution to custom instrument support.
Official project: https://ebbr.neocities.org/
History and release notes: https://ebbr.neocities.org/newsroom_content
